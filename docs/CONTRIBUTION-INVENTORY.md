# AX23 v2 contribution inventory (`v0.6-wifi-integration`)

Baseline: `Sply-Dev/openwrt-archer-ax23-v2` `main` at `c4fe9ad` (which includes patches `0001`–`0005` plus `0006-qualcommax-ipq50xx-uniphy-pcs-preconfig.patch` from v0.5). The external engineering source evaluated here is [`john35413/openwrt-archer-ax23-v2-build`](https://github.com/john35413/openwrt-archer-ax23-v2-build) (`dev` at `ed48a54`) and PR #2 ([`Sply-Dev/openwrt-archer-ax23-v2#2`](https://github.com/Sply-Dev/openwrt-archer-ax23-v2/pull/2)).

Because `patches/0006-qualcommax-ipq50xx-uniphy-pcs-preconfig.patch` occupies slot `0006` on `main`, the seven patches from PR #2 are renumbered `0007` through `0013` on `v0.6-wifi-integration` (with the `rproc_shutdown()`/`RPROC_CRASHED` refcount fix folded into `0012`), followed by four fast-win patches (`0014` through `0017`) selected from the 27-patch standalone tree.

**Evidence boundary:** `v0.5-test` has physical hardware validation for RAM boot, the 2.5 Gb/s HSGMII switch trunk (`EXT0/SDS0`), LAN-to-WAN routing (445 Mbps down / 100 Mbps up), factory MAC extraction from `tp_data`, LEDs, and `tp_boot_idx` A/B slot inspection. The `v0.6-wifi-integration` patches (`0007`–`0017`) are host-verified (`[VERIFIED]` via source audit and `tests/host/` models) and awaiting physical hardware testing alongside the locally generated `board-2.bin` containers.
